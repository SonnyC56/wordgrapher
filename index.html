How It Works

Every visualization is generated in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

1. Convert

We map each character to an ASCII value (or morse symbols) to build visual patterns.

2. Style

Adjust colors, smoothing, and layouts to match the vibe you want.

3. Download

Export PNG images and share via a custom URL with your text embedded.

FAQ

Quick answers about Word Grapher.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser, and no text is stored on a server.

Can I share a visualization?

Yes. Use “Copy Share Link” to generate a URL that reloads your text on any device.

What texts work best?

Short phrases show the strongest visual rhythm, but long passages work too.

Can I download the output?

Every visualization includes a one-click PNG download for easy sharing.

Which visualization types are available?

ASCII line, circular spiral, morse art, barcode sweep, heatmap, constellation, waveform, chord diagram, and bubble frequency.
